1. Privacy & Data Protection Policy

OmniMail processes email recipient data strictly on behalf of our enterprise customers. We never sell, monetize, or share recipient email lists, contact attributes, or telemetry data with third-party advertising networks.

All data is encrypted at rest within our SQLite WAL engine and transmitted securely over TLS 1.3. Customers retain full ownership and right of deletion over all audience data.
